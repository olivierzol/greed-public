How to play

🎲

Roll & stack

Flick to roll two dice. Their sum piles onto the round pot. Keep rolling to grow it — or bank.

💰

Bank to keep

Banking adds the pot to your score and passes the phone. Un-banked pots vanish if you bust.

⚠️

Seven busts

Roll a total of 7 and the pot burns. Your turn is over — bank before your luck runs out.

🎯

Doubles double

Matching dice pay double — but you MUST roll again. Three doubles in a row and the pot is wiped.

🐍

Snake eyes

Double ones wipe your entire banked score back to zero. Brutal.

🏁

Break 100 to win

The first player past 100 triggers a final round — highest score at the end wins. Land exactly 100 and you reset!

Three ways to roll

Simple

Steady needle, fixed zones. Time your flick — pure skill.

Crazy

Zones and needle speed reshuffle every throw. Nervous fun.

Shake & Throw

Shake the phone, then throw — chuck it too hard and it flies off the table.
