Frequently asked

How many players?

2 to 8, sharing one device — pass the phone on each turn.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Greed runs entirely offline. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

How do I win?

Be the first past 100 to trigger the final round — then hold the highest banked score when it ends. Landing on exactly 100 resets you to zero, so aim to overshoot!

What is Shake & Throw?

An optional roll mode: shake the phone like dice in your hand, then throw. A gentle toss is safe; chuck it too hard and the dice fly off the table. It uses your device's motion sensor and needs no permissions beyond the one-time motion prompt.

My progress disappeared

Game progress is stored only on your device and is cleared when a game ends or you return to the home screen. There is no cloud backup by design — your data never leaves your phone.
