Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Greed does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Greed is a local, pass-the-phone dice game. Everything happens on your device:

  • No account or sign-up of any kind.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No network connection is used or required — the game runs fully offline.
  • Player names and game scores are saved only on your device (so you can resume a game) and are removed when the game ends or you start over.

Device sensors

The optional Shake & Throw roll mode reads your device's motion sensor (accelerometer) only while you are actively rolling, purely to detect the throw. This motion data is used in the moment and never stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

Children's privacy

Greed is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
